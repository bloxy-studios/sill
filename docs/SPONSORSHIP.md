# Sponsorship Policy

Rules that apply to any future sponsorship of Sill. Written before any
sponsor exists so the terms never bend to fit a check. Current sponsors:
**none**. Current funding: **none**. (Status lives in [FUNDING.md](FUNDING.md).)

## What sponsorship is

Money that sustains development: maintainer time, cross-platform test
hardware, signing certificates, CI/infrastructure. Allocation transparency:
[FUNDING.md](FUNDING.md).

## What sponsors receive

- Recognition: name/logo in a SPONSORS section (README/site) sized by tier,
  and thanks in release notes for the period sponsored
- The same software, releases, and support as everyone else
- A maintainer who can spend more hours on the project — that's the product

## What sponsors do not receive — non-negotiable

- **No governance rights.** Technical decisions follow
  [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md) regardless of funding.
- **No roadmap placement.** Sponsoring doesn't reorder priorities; a sponsor
  wanting a feature argues for it in public like anyone else.
- **No preferential security treatment.** No early advisory access, no
  private-fix priority. Security handling is identical for all users.
- **No ownership or license privileges.** Sill stays MIT OR Apache-2.0 for
  everyone; there is no "sponsor edition."
- **No endorsement.** Recognition is gratitude, not Sill endorsing a
  sponsor's products.

## Refusal and removal

Sponsorship may be declined or returned when association would damage the
project (conduct, legal risk, or attempts to leverage sponsorship against the
rules above). Recognition is removed when sponsorship lapses; nothing else
changes, because nothing else was for sale.

## Corporate sponsors

Same rules, larger logos. A company that needs contractual terms, support
SLAs, or feature guarantees is describing a commercial relationship, not
sponsorship — see [SUSTAINABILITY.md](SUSTAINABILITY.md) for how that would be
kept separate from the open-source project.
