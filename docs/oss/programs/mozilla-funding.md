# Mozilla (MOSS and Mozilla Technology Fund)

Status: CLOSED (Last verified: 2026-08-21)

## Program

Two separate Mozilla grantmaking vehicles, both currently shut to applicants. Mozilla Open Source Support (MOSS) funded open source infrastructure across three tracks (Foundational Technology, Mission Partners, Secure Open Source) until late 2020. The Mozilla Technology Fund (MTF), run by Mozilla Foundation from 2022, funded open source work on AI accountability, then AI auditing tools, then AI and environmental justice (2024 cohort). Neither is accepting applications.

## Official URL

https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/moss/ and https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/what-we-fund/programs/mozilla-technology-fund-mtf/

## What it funds

MOSS: open source projects Mozilla relied on or that broadened access, security and user empowerment. MTF: open source technologies adding accountability, transparency and user agency to the AI ecosystem, themed per call.

## Eligibility

MOSS: not applicable, hiatus. MTF, from its published theory of change while active: free software/OSI or FSF licence required; projects "generally must have at least a working prototype in hand"; a core team must already be in place with a track record; the fund "generally does not fund projects which are at the idea stage"; preference for values-driven teams shifting power, and encouragement for applicants from the Global Majority and other under-represented groups.

## Typical funding

MOSS Foundational Technology track (historic): $10,000 minimum, $250,000 maximum, generally one-off awards for a defined piece of work. MTF (historic): up to $50,000 each plus one year of mentorship. These are archival figures — nothing is currently on offer.

## Application requirements

None available. Mozilla's own "What We Fund" page states: "We are not currently accepting any applications for funding." MOSS's page says the program is "on indefinite hiatus." Mozilla has signalled a future grantmaking strategy with more emphasis on sustainability, mentorship and long-term maintenance, but has published no open call.

## Sill fit

NOT ELIGIBLE. Two independent blockers: nothing is open, and the thematic fit is poor. MTF funds tools that hold AI systems accountable — auditing, transparency, bias, environmental impact. Sill is developer tooling that happens to work alongside coding agents; that is not the same thing and a reviewer would not stretch to it. Do not attempt to reframe Sill as an AI-accountability project.

## Missing evidence

- An open call to apply to. Nothing else matters until then.

## Application strategy

Monitor mozillafoundation.org/en/what-we-fund/ once or twice a year for the announced "next era" of grantmaking. Do not invest preparation time. If a maintenance/sustainability-themed fund launches, re-read the criteria then.

## Deadline

None. MOSS closed to new applications since late 2020; MTF's last cohort was 2024.
