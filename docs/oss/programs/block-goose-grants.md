# goose grant program (Block / Agentic AI Foundation)

Status: CURRENT — funded, but review process being revised (Last verified: 2026-08-21)

## Program

Grant program launched by Block in July 2025 to fund external contributors building on goose, Block's open source AI agent platform. In December 2025 goose became a project of the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) under the Linux Foundation. Following layoffs at Block, the goose maintainers stated on GitHub that "The goose grant program remains funded and will continue as before, though we are updating our processes for assessing and accepting new proposals."

## Official URL

https://block.xyz/inside/introducing-the-goose-grant-program (application form referenced from the goose repo discussions: https://forms.gle/psHBhj8xFY8q1nHP7)

## What it funds

Work that expands what is possible with agentic AI via goose. Named interest areas: new interaction paradigms (sketch, voice, multi-modal), autonomous background operation, self-improving agents, real-world automation, new MCP integrations, security audits and developer tooling around the goose ecosystem.

## Eligibility

- Individuals and/or small teams.
- The work must not already be funded by an employer or client.
- Proposals must demonstrate an open-source-first mindset and align with goose's values.
- Reviewed in rolling cohorts; grants are milestone-based with periodic check-ins, deliverables and reviews.

## Typical funding

Up to $100,000 per grant over 12 months, paid against milestones. This is the largest single figure on this research list, which is precisely why the scope constraint below matters.

## Application requirements

Google Form proposal, submitted at any time. Questions can be raised in the goose Discord #goose-grants forum. Expect the intake process to change — the maintainers have said they will publish clearer timelines, criteria and status visibility as the revised process settles.

## Sill fit

LOW. The money is real but it funds goose-specific work, and Sill's stated thesis is provider neutrality across Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and others. Writing a proposal that is honestly goose-centric would distort Sill's design; writing one that is not goose-centric will not be funded. The only defensible version is a narrowly scoped proposal for a first-class goose integration in Sill — a real deliverable that benefits goose users — funded as a component, not a proposal to build Sill. Do not pitch the whole product.

## Missing evidence

- A working terminal with a session/agent abstraction that a goose integration could plug into.
- An actual goose integration prototype, or at minimum a credible technical design for one.
- Users who would benefit, so the "who is this for" question has an answer.
- Confirmation the revised review process is live and what its criteria are.

## Application strategy

Defer until Sill runs agents at all. Then consider a scoped, milestone-based proposal for goose support, sized realistically (a fraction of the $100k ceiling), and be transparent that Sill is multi-provider — the AAIF/Linux Foundation governance move makes vendor-neutrality more acceptable, not less. Re-check the program's status before writing anything; it is in transition.

## Deadline

None — rolling submissions, cohort review.
