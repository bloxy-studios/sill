# Prototype Fund (Germany)

Status: SEASONAL — Class 03 applications 2026-10-01 to 2026-11-30 (Last verified: 2026-08-21)

## Program

German public funding programme for open source software in the public interest, financed by the federal government and administered for individual developers and small teams. Focus areas are software infrastructure and data security. Since the 2024 restructuring it runs one application phase and one funding class per year: a six-month funding period starting 1 June, with an optional four-month second stage to the following March.

## Official URL

https://www.prototypefund.de/en/funding (FAQ: https://www.prototypefund.de/en/faq)

## What it funds

Building a software prototype from an idea, over six months. Public-interest open source only; funding priorities are software infrastructure and data security. The software must be published under an open source license with publicly accessible code.

## Eligibility

Hard residency and legal-form constraints:

- Individual developers: must be resident in Germany, self-employed or freelance, and pay taxes in Germany.
- Teams (max four people): must form a Gesellschaft bürgerlichen Rechts (GbR) after jury selection. No other legal form is permitted. The GbR's registered office and the managing director's residence must be in Germany; other team members may reside elsewhere in the EU. All funded members must be registered EU residents.
- No payouts to companies, associations, universities or other institutions.
- Applicant must be of legal age. Applications accepted in German or English.
- Subcontracting to third parties is not permitted.

## Typical funding

Up to €158,000 was stated for Class 02 (from the federal ministry). Confirm the figure for Class 03 when the round opens — do not assume it carries over.

## Application requirements

Complete online application on the Prototype Fund platform within the window; 15 application questions. Jury selection, then a formal GbR application for teams, then ministry review of the formal application. A GbR template is provided only after selection.
