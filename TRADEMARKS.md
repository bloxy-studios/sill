# Name and Brand Policy

"Sill" is the name of this project. It is not a registered trademark, and no
trademark registration is claimed at this time. This policy exists to keep the
name useful and unambiguous, not to restrict legitimate use.

## You may, without asking

- Use "Sill" to refer to this project, factually, in articles, talks,
  packaging metadata, and documentation.
- State that your software "works with Sill" or "integrates with Sill" when it
  does.
- Distribute unmodified builds of Sill under the name "Sill" with a clear
  pointer back to this repository.

## Please don't

- Name a fork, derivative, or unrelated product "Sill" or something confusingly
  similar (e.g. "Sill Pro", "SillTerm") without permission. Forks should pick
  their own name and may state they are "based on Sill".
- Imply endorsement by, or affiliation with, the Sill project without
  permission.
- Use the name in a way that suggests your modified build is the official one.

## Logo

Sill does not yet have a logo. When one exists, it will be added to this
repository with its usage terms noted here.
